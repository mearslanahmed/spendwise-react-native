Account and Data Deletion

How to delete your account in the app:

  1. Open the SpendWise app.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap on the Settings icon (Gear icon).
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account & Clear Data.
  5. Confirm your decision. This will permanently delete your account, wallets, transactions, budgets, and all associated data.

Request deletion via email:

If you no longer have the app installed, you can request account deletion by emailing us at:

spendwiseoffical@gmail.com

Please send the email from the address associated with your SpendWise account with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will process your request within 7 business days.
